# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: May 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how auto-sns-poster collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application.

## 1. Information We Collect
When you authenticate our application with your TikTok account, we may collect minimal information required to provide the core functionality of the service. This includes:
- Your TikTok Profile Information (such as username)
- Video content and metadata authorized for upload

## 2. How We Use Your Information
The collected information is used **strictly** for the purpose of operating the application, specifically to post videos to your TikTok account on your behalf. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
We do not permanently store your TikTok authentication tokens or personal profile data on public servers. Access tokens are kept securely and used solely for executing API requests.

## 4. Data Deletion Instructions
You have the right to revoke our application's access to your data at any time. To do this, you can remove auto-sns-poster from the authorized apps list within your TikTok account settings. Upon revocation, we will immediately cease access to your account and delete any temporary data associated with it.
